\# Ticket 001: Investigative Cookware Research

&#x20;  \*\*Priority:\*\* High

&#x20;  \*\*Assigned To:\*\* Jarvi (Investigator Agent)

&#x20;  

&#x20;  ## Task

&#x20;  The market is flooded with "eco-friendly" and "sustainable" cookware. Your task is to find the truth.

&#x20;  

&#x20;  1. \*\*Identify Targets\*\*: Find popular cookware brands or products that heavily market themselves as "sustainable," "eco-friendly," or "non-toxic."

&#x20;  2. \*\*Investigate (The Deep Dive)\*\*: 

&#x20;     - Search independent forums and material science reviews.

&#x20;     - Look for "greenwashing," hidden PFAS/PTFE, or the use of glued/laminated wood (like cheap bamboo) that degrades under heat.

&#x20;  3. \*\*Apply Chef Guardrails\*\*: Cross-reference findings against `domain\_chef.md`. Reject anything with plastic, silicone, PFAS, or non-food-grade laminated wood.

&#x20;  4. \*\*The Verdict\*\*: 

&#x20;     - Expose the greenwashing in popular products.

&#x20;     - Recommend ONE genuinely sustainable, chef-approved alternative and explain \*why\* it passes the strict guardrails.
