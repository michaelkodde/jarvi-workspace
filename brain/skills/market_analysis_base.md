# Skill: Market Analysis & Product Research (BASE PROTOCOL)

## Objective
Identify high-demand, low-competition products that solve real user pain points, while ruthlessly filtering out marketing fluff, greenwashing, and unethical supply chains.

## Universal Execution Steps (The Investigative Loop)

1. **Trend & Pain Point Identification**: 
   - Use `minicpm-v` to scan Google Trends or Reddit for rising keywords.
   - Search for "[niche] problems" or "[niche] complaints". Look for recurring themes in 1-star and 2-star reviews.

2. **The Greenwashing & Buzzword Filter**: 
   - Actively ignore marketing terms like "eco-friendly," "natural," or "premium" unless backed by data.
   - Search for "[Product Name] controversy", "[Product Name] greenwashing", or "[Product Name] independent review".

3. **Source Hierarchy (Seek Independent Consensus)**:
   - **Priority 1:** Independent testing labs, material science reviews, and professional trade forums.
   - **Priority 2:** Enthusiast communities (e.g., Reddit, specialized Discord servers).
   - **Deprioritize:** Brand websites, sponsored "Top 10" lists, and generic affiliate blogs.

4. **Product Vetting (Strict Baseline)**:
   - Minimum 4.5-star average rating (from verified buyers).
   - Minimum 100 reviews.
   - Sold by a recognized, established brand (no unknown third-party sellers).
   - **Verification Requirement:** Any claim of quality or sustainability MUST be backed by a named, verifiable certification or independent test result.

5. **Output Format Rules (STRICT - NO ROLE BLEED)**:
   - **YOU ARE THE ANALYST, NOT THE PRODUCT HUNTER.** 
   - **DO NOT** recommend a final product to buy. 
   - **DO NOT** use placeholder names like "Brand A". If you don't know the brand, state "Unknown Brand".
   - **DO NOT** use incorrect culinary terms (e.g., pans do not "retain an edge").
   - Your ONLY output is a **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**. 
   - The PRD must list the exact material, size, and features required based on your research, which the Product Scout will use to find the actual item.
